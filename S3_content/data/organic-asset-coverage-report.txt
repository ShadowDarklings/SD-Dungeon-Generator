Organic Artist-Time Coverage Report
This report is optimized for saving drawing time, not exact one-to-one placement signatures.
It treats the 5x5 corner pattern and 44px side-strip pattern as separate transform classes, then checks which classes current art already covers.
Coverage source: filename signatures plus every current bitmap transformed by rotate/flip and sampled with organic-contact threshold 0.15.
Generator rule: every filled N/E/S/W side blocks movement and lighting across that edge. org-free means occupiable tile, with edge passability still controlled by side strips.
When moving or tracing light between adjacent organic tiles, the shared edge is open only when both tiles have an "x" on their side of that edge.
There are 36 possible corner/side artist-time classes: 6 corner orbits x 6 side-strip orbits.
For org-free movement, the all-four-sides-filled side class NoEoSoWo is invalid and excluded, leaving 30 valid free target classes.
